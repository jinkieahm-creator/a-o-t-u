LAYERING | OCT 2026

How to Layer for In-Between Weather

Start light, add a long coat, and keep the proportions relaxed. Three simple rules for mornings that turn warm by noon.

Layer a thin knit under a long coat, then add a light scarf you can pull off by noon. Keep the shapes loose, so each piece moves freely when the temperature shifts.

STYLE GUIDE | OCT 2026

Five Ways to Wear a Pleated Skirt

From soft knits to structured jackets, the pleated skirt is the easiest piece to build a week of outfits around.

Try it with a chunky knit in the morning, a tucked shirt and a structured jacket for the office, and a plain tee with sneakers on the weekend. One skirt, five different moods.

COLOR NOTES | SEP 2026

Pale Green & Butter Yellow

Two quiet colors that brighten neutral wardrobes without trying too hard. Here is how we pair them this season.

Pale green works best against cream and navy, while butter yellow glows next to grey and brown. Keep one of them as the accent and let everything else stay quiet.
